# ALMA — Use Cases

## Persona 1: First Encounter
Who: Marina, 34. Dances tango for a year and a half.
Context: Saw the app mentioned in her tango school chat.
Device: iPhone 15. 10:40 pm, at home, after a milonga.

Step 1. Opens the app. Sees the word "Alma" on a black screen.
        Pause. A line appears: "Tango is not what you dance..."
        Does not tap for three seconds. Reads.

Step 2. Onboarding. Four slides. Does not skip. On the third slide
        ("No account. No internet. No notifications.") smiles slightly.

Step 3. Main screen. Three lines. Taps "Respirar".

Step 4. Chooses Compás mode. Duration: 2 minutes (not sure yet).

Step 5. Breathes. The point expands and contracts. First 20 seconds
        she is distracted. Then catches the rhythm. The hold on count 5
        feels uncomfortably long. Wants to inhale earlier. Does not.

Step 6. Session ends. Darkness. A line from "Yira, yira" appears.
        Marina does not know the word "grela". Taps. Goes to Leer.

Step 7. Reads the text line by line. Taps on "grela", "yira", "morfar".
        Three words go into her dictionary. The text takes four minutes.

Step 8. End. "Breathe with this text" option. Taps.
        Abrazo mode. 5 minutes. For the first time all evening
        she feels she has exhaled.

Step 9. Closes the app. Receives no notifications.
        Nothing asks for a review. Nothing sells anything.

Result: 12 minutes in the app. Three new words. One poem.
        Two breathing cycles. Zero irritation.


## Persona 2: Morning Ritual
Who: Alexey, 48. Dances for four years. Teaches beginners.
Context: Every morning, 7:15, before work. Coffee is not ready yet.
Device: Samsung Galaxy S24.

Step 1. Opens Alma. Skips the main screen — taps "Respirar" directly.

Step 2. Pausa mode. 10 minutes. This is his ritual: sits in the kitchen,
        phone on the table, watches the point. Holds have grown to
        8 seconds. Two months ago 5 felt like eternity.

Step 3. End. A line from "Sur". Reads it. Taps "Home".

Step 4. Goes to Hablar. Word of the day: "curda". Does not know it.
        Reads the card. Etymology: from Quechua. Did not expect that.
        Adds to dictionary.

Step 5. Quiz. 10 questions. Gets two wrong. Not upset.
        After each error reads the etymology. Remembers better
        than if he had answered correctly.

Step 6. Closes the app. 14 minutes. Every day. Third month running.

Result: Breathing practice has become part of the morning.
        Lunfardo grows by 2-3 words a day. The quiz is not a test
        but a way to learn something new.


## Persona 3: Preparing for a Trip
Who: Dasha, 29. Dances for two years. Flying to Buenos Aires in a month.
Context: Evening, was planning her route on a laptop. Opened Alma on phone.
Device: iPhone SE.

Step 1. Goes to Hablar -> Buenos Aires Map.

Step 2. San Telmo: 41 of 60 words. La Boca: 12 of 50. Abasto: 3 of 50.
        Decides to build up La Boca — the neighborhood she will visit first.

Step 3. Browses La Boca words. Many Italian roots.
        "Laburar", "morfar", "fainá". Reads etymologies.
        Understands why tango has so much Italian melody.

Step 4. At the 25-word milestone a quote from "La cumparsita" appears.
        Taps. Goes to Leer. Reads the text. Understands more than
        a month ago because she knows the words.

Step 5. Returns to Hablar. Takes a quiz on La Boca words.
        9 out of 10.

Step 6. Before bed: Respirar, Caminar mode, 5 minutes.
        Breathes and imagines walking through San Telmo.

Result: 25 minutes. The app has become part of trip preparation.
        Does not replace lessons but provides cultural context
        not found in tango textbooks.


## Persona 4: After a Hard Milonga
Who: Thomas, 52. Dances for seven years.
Context: 1:30 am. Returned from a milonga. Danced but felt tense.
         Could not find the pause. Rushed.
Device: Pixel 8.

Step 1. Does not open social media or email. Opens Alma.

Step 2. Main screen. Three lines. Does not think. Taps "Respirar".

Step 3. Pausa mode. 15 minutes. The longest one. Sits on the edge
        of the bed. Phone in hand. The point breathes.

Step 4. First two minutes his thoughts race. "Why did I rush.
        Why didn't I wait. Why was I afraid of the pause."

Step 5. By the fourth minute breathing evens out. The 10-second hold
        stops being scary. The point freezes — and he freezes.
        Does not breathe. Waits. Like in tango.

Step 6. End. A line: "Sur" by Manzi. Does not tap. Just reads.
        "Barrio de San Juan..." Closes his eyes.

Step 7. Taps "Home". Does not go to Leer. Does not go to Hablar.
        Just closes the app. Goes to sleep.

Result: 17 minutes. Not learning. Not a quiz. Not progress.
        Just the silence that was missing at the milonga.


## Persona 5: Curiosity Without Tango
Who: Irina, 41. Does not dance. Hispanist. Interested in Latin
     American culture.
Context: Heard the word "lunfardo" in a podcast. Googled it. Found Alma.
Device: iPad Air.

Step 1. Onboarding. Reads carefully. The word "lunfardo" is familiar,
        but the tango context is not.

Step 2. Goes straight to Hablar. Not Respirar. Not Leer. The word.

Step 3. Browses cards. "Grela", "gotán", "curda", "yeta".
        Etymologies: Italian, Quechua, French, Latin.
        As a philologist, gets hooked on the mutation chains.

Step 4. Quiz. Takes it like a game. 10 questions. 7 correct.

Step 5. Goes to Leer. Reads "Cambalache" — a satire on the 1930s.
        The context card explains the political background.
        Taps on words. Understands that the text is not "poetry
        for beauty" but an angry political commentary.

Step 6. Does not breathe. Does not dance. But closes the app feeling
        she understood something about Buenos Aires that was not
        in textbooks.

Result: 20 minutes. The app works not only for dancers.
        Lunfardo and poetry are a standalone value.


## Persona 6: Re-Reading
Who: Marina (from Persona 1). Three weeks later.
Context: Sunday, 4:00 pm. Rain. Nowhere to go.

Step 1. Opens Alma. Goes to Leer.

Step 2. Re-reads "Yira, yira". The second time she notices words
        she missed: "fatiga", "mishadura". Taps. Adds them.

Step 3. After the text — "Breathe with this text". Abrazo mode. 5 minutes.
        Breathes and repeats to herself the lines she remembered.

Step 4. Goes to Hablar. Quiz. Words from "Yira, yira" appear in questions.
        Answers correctly. Not because she studied — because she read
        them in context.

Result: Re-reading reveals new layers. The app does not end after
        the first read. Texts are re-read like poems: each time
        you find a new word.


## Use Case Summary

    Persona              Modules             Time     Goal
    --------------------------------------------------------------
    Marina (first time)  R -> L -> R         12 min   Get acquainted
    Alexey (morning)     R -> H (quiz)       14 min   Ritual, habit
    Dasha (trip prep)    H (map) -> L -> R   25 min   Preparation, context
    Thomas (after)       R                   17 min   Silence, exhale
    Irina (philologist)  H -> L              20 min   Language, culture
    Marina (re-read)     L -> R -> H         15 min   Re-reading, depth

    R = Respirar. H = Hablar. L = Leer.
